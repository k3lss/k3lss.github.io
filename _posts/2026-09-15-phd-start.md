I have started my PhD at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid, in the Departamento de Física de la Tierra y Astrofísica
and as part of [GUAIX](https://guaix.ucm.es/), under the supervision of Dr. Shane P. O'Sullivan.
I will use the polarized emission of radio galaxies to study cosmic birefringence.
