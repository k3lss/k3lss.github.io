I presented the poster *The lack of PNG response of zero-bias tracers* at the
[XI Meeting on Fundamental Cosmology](https://cosmo2025.unican.es/) in Santander (18–20 November 2025),
and gave a flash talk about it in the session on Wednesday 19 November.
My attendance was supported by a grant from the Instituto de Física de Cantabria (IFCA), the organizer of the meeting.
You can find the poster and the slides on the [Talks & Posters]({{ '/talks/' | relative_url }}) page.
