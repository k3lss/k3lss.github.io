title: "Paper published in Astronomy & Astrophysics"
---

Our paper *Unclustered tracers remain unclustered: the lack of primordial non-Gaussianity response of bias-zero tracers*
has been published in *Astronomy & Astrophysics* as a Letter.
We show that tracers with zero linear bias, selected by their local density, do not respond to primordial non-Gaussianity.
[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202558825) or see more on my [research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).
