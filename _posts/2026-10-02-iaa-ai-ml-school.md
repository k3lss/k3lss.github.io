title: "IAA-CSIC Severo Ochoa School on AI and Machine Learning in Astronomy"
---

I attended the [IAA-CSIC Severo Ochoa School on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Astronomy](https://www.granadacongresos.com/ai-ml)
at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Andalucía in Granada (28 September – 2 October 2026).
The school covered machine learning applications across astronomy, including morphological classification of radio sources
with CNNs and Vision Transformers, graph neural networks on dark matter halo simulations, deep learning reconstruction for
Cherenkov telescopes, unsupervised clustering for chemical tagging of star clusters, and image reconstruction for sparse
interferometric arrays.
